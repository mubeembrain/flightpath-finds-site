Flightpath Finds - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Who we are

Flightpath Finds is a private publishing tool run by Ben Goode in Melbourne, Australia. It posts short World of Warcraft videos to the Flightpath Finds accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, and reads how those videos perform. It is used only on accounts its owner controls. It is not offered to the public and no one else signs in to it.

What the tool accesses

  • Basic account details (account name and ID) for the connected Flightpath Finds accounts.
  • Permission to upload videos, titles and captions to those accounts.
  • The videos' own statistics (views, watch time, likes, shares, comments and similar counts).
  • Comments left on those videos, to read what viewers say.

It does not access any other person's account, contacts, messages or private data.

How the data is used

Only to post the owner's videos and to judge which videos work better, so future videos improve. Data is never sold, rented, shared with advertisers or used to build profiles of viewers.

Where the data is kept

Access tokens and statistics are stored on the owner's own computer and in the owner's private records. Tokens are never published. Statistics are kept while they are useful for that purpose and can be deleted at any time.

Third parties

The tool talks only to the platforms' own APIs:

  • YouTube: Flightpath Finds uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Instagram and Facebook: Meta Privacy Policy.
  • TikTok: TikTok Privacy Policy.

Flightpath Finds' use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Google and YouTube: remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Instagram and Facebook: remove the app under Settings, then Apps and websites.
  • TikTok: remove the app under Settings and privacy, then Security, then Apps and services permissions.

To ask for any stored data to be deleted, email the contact below. It is removed from the current records within 30 days; older copies stay only in the owner's private, access-controlled version history and are never used again.
